Frequently asked questions

Why is my shop pending review?

Email confirmation gives you access to your account. Business verification is a separate review of your shop information and TIN. This workspace shows the status saved for your shop.

How do I help customers find my shop?

Add a recognisable shop name, clear premises photos, search phrases and an exact location in Shop profile.

How can I correct my TIN?

The TIN is locked after registration. Email dukafind@gmail.com with your shop username and an explanation of the correction needed.

How do I update a product?

Open Products and choose Edit beside the item. Update its photo, description or price, then save your changes.

Where do customer messages appear?

Open Messages to see your conversations. Select a customer to reply with text or an image.
