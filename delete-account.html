Delete your account

Request deletion of your Duka Find account and associated data. This page is available outside the app and without an active sign-in.

What happens to your information

The team verifies account ownership, then removes the account and associated active-service data: your profile, owned shop and products, owned uploads, applicable conversations/messages, favourites and block/terms records. Removing a conversation may also remove it from the other participant’s history.

Minimal safety, moderation or legal records may be retained for legitimate reasons described in the Privacy Policy. Backup copies follow the configured provider retention schedule. This is a request process, not an instant deletion button.
