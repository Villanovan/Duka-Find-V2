Duka Find is operated by Bitware Tech Solutions, Uganda. Contact the developers at dukafind@gmail.com. Duka Find helps people discover local shops, view listings, message shop owners and open map directions.

All customer and shop-owner accounts are for people aged 18 or over. You must confirm this before creating or using an account. Google sign-in does not establish your age. Do not create an account for a minor. Shop owners must also be legally authorised to represent their business.

Give accurate information, keep your credentials secure and contact support about suspected unauthorised access. Do not impersonate another person or business or use another person's account without permission. You are responsible for activity you authorise through your account.

4. Shops and verification

Owners must have the right to advertise their business and supply accurate business, verification, location, product and delivery information. Keep prices, availability and opening information up to date. A verification badge describes the status recorded by Duka Find; it is not a guarantee of a seller's conduct, product quality or legal compliance.

5. Purchases and enquiries

Owners are responsible for their products, lawful sales, descriptions, prices, taxes, delivery, warranties and refunds. Customers should confirm stock, the full price, delivery or collection and return arrangements before paying. A message does not reserve stock or complete a purchase.

Duka Find currently provides discovery and communication tools. It does not operate an in-app checkout, hold purchase funds or fulfil orders. Direct purchases are between the customer and shop. Mandatory consumer rights and the parties' legal responsibilities still apply.

6. Prohibited use and content

Do not use Duka Find for scams, fraud, impersonation, illegal sales, stolen or counterfeit goods, misleading claims, harassment, threats, hate, discriminatory abuse, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery or child sexual abuse material. Do not publish another person's private information without a lawful basis.

Weapons, explosives, illegal drugs, controlled medicines without lawful authorisation, gambling services and adult sexual services must not be listed. You retain ownership of your content and permit Duka Find to store, display and transmit it to operate the features you use. Public shop details and photos are visible to visitors; chat content is shared with conversation participants and handled by the service infrastructure. Operator access is limited to legitimate service, support, security and moderation purposes described in the Privacy Policy. Chat is not represented as end-to-end encrypted.

8. Reports, blocking and enforcement

Use the in-app Report and Block controls or contact dukafind@gmail.com. Reports are reviewed; they do not automatically remove content. Blocking stops new messages between the two accounts. Existing messages may remain visible, and unblocking permits messaging only if neither account has blocked the other.

Bitware Tech Solutions may remove or hide prohibited content, restrict accounts and investigate abuse. Urgent safety or security concerns may require action without advance notice. To ask for a review of a decision, email support with the relevant shop or case reference. Do not send passwords or verification codes. This is not an emergency service.

Pins and straight-line distances can be inaccurate or out of date. Start opens Google Maps; its terms and privacy practices apply to that service. Check the destination, follow local conditions and do not operate the app while driving.
