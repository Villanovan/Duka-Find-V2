Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-02 · Bitware Tech Solutions, Uganda.

Who operates Duka Find

Bitware Tech Solutions, Uganda operates Duka Find. Contact dukafind@gmail.com for privacy, access, correction, deletion, security or moderation enquiries.

Adults only

Duka Find accounts are for adults aged 18 or over. We collect an adulthood declaration and the accepted terms version/time rather than obtaining a birth date from Google. Google sign-in does not verify age for Duka Find. If you believe an under-18 account has been created, contact support so it can be investigated and handled appropriately.

Information used and why

  • Account details: identifiers, email and name from Google sign-in or email registration; owner profile details supplied at signup. Used for authentication, account operation and support. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Shop and verification information: business name, username, category, TIN, location/coordinates, delivery text, search phrases and premises photos. Used to maintain listings and business verification. TINs and profile emails are excluded from public column access by access controls; the owner and authorised operator functions handle them.
  • Public listings: products, descriptions, prices, photos, shop details and public shop coordinates. Displayed so customers can discover and contact businesses. Do not put private personal information into public fields.
  • Conversations: participant identifiers, messages, attached images, timestamps and read markers. Used to deliver chat and its read status. Chat is not end-to-end encrypted. Authorised support/moderation work may require access.
  • Location: an optional browser location request is used to sort nearby shops and calculate approximate straight-line distance. The website uses it for the current search; it does not add a location-history table. Map and browser providers may still process related network information.
  • Safety and requests: terms/adult attestations, blocks, reports, moderation decisions and deletion-request references. Used to apply user choices, review abuse, enforce policies and process requests.
  • Technical data and browser storage: service requests can expose IP addresses and technical information to hosting, authentication, CDN and map providers. Browser storage is used for authentication, favourites/setup state, temporary signup completion, terms handoff and workspace preferences. Clearing browser data may remove unsaved local setup. No advertising or analytics SDK is added to this service; providers' necessary service logs may still exist.

Who receives information

Public listings are visible to visitors. Chat content is shared with its participants. The service uses Supabase for authentication, database and uploaded-file storage; GitHub Pages for website hosting; Geoapify/OpenStreetMap for map display; and external font/CDN services used by the website. Google receives information when you choose Google sign-in or open Google Maps. Network requests to these providers can disclose technical information, including IP addresses, request metadata or map areas.

We do not claim that all processing takes place in Uganda. Hosting/processor locations and contracts must be assessed by the operator for lawful cross-border processing. This policy does not promise that an external provider has no independent obligations or data handling; review its own notices as relevant.

Permissions and choices

You can decline location permission and still browse/search pinned shops. Camera capture and file selection are user initiated; choosing an image does not authorise access to the rest of your library. Product photos upload on Save, shop photos upload when chosen, and chat images upload when sent. Device/browser permission settings control access. Denying a permission limits the corresponding feature, not your ability to contact support.

Retention and deletion

Account, shop and product information is kept while needed to operate the account and service. Messages and files are kept to provide conversations and support. Request deletion at the public deletion page or through account/support links. Requests require ownership verification and are queued for the operator; sending one is not an immediate deletion confirmation.

The account-deletion process removes the requesting account's active profile, shop/catalogue data, favourites, message/conversation records covered by the request, its terms/block records, owned uploaded files and authentication account. Conversation removal can affect the other participant's chat history. Independently owned content belonging to other users is not deleted merely because it was linked by the requester. Any such content still identifying you can be raised with support.

Minimal report/moderation records may be retained to investigate abuse, handle disputes or meet legal obligations. The requester's report text and identity are cleared by the account-deletion process where applicable; case references, reasons, target references and action records may remain for legitimate case handling. Operators must review these records and remove or de-identify them when no longer needed. Backup copies may remain until overwritten under the configured provider backup schedule; they should not be used for ordinary service after a deletion, and deletions must be reapplied if data is restored.

We review the scope of each request, associated files and any lawful retention exception before marking a deletion completed. Support will communicate request status and any lawful retention exception. No fixed response time or provider backup period is invented by this policy.

Security and access

Our design uses HTTPS service endpoints, authenticated sessions, database access rules, private moderator membership and server-only administration credentials. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Moderation and deletion require an authorised operator; a normal user cannot grant themselves those permissions. Do not send passwords or verification codes by email.

Your requests and complaints

Email dukafind@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or information about data handling, to report a suspected security issue, or to challenge a decision. We may reasonably verify account ownership before disclosing or changing data. You may also contact Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office about your rights. Nothing in this policy restricts rights available under applicable law.

Changes

Material policy changes will be communicated appropriately. Bitware Tech Solutions must keep this policy, its actual service configuration and Google Play Data safety answers consistent.
